# 28. Capstone Projects and Portfolio

Integrate architecture, implementation, evaluation, security, governance, and communication into portfolio evidence. A shared rubric keeps five possible capstones comparable while allowing different tools and domains.

## Before you begin

Complete the modules required by your chosen projects and review the [capstone rules and rubric](01-capstone-rules-and-rubric.md). Use synthetic or public data, document every dependency, and choose projects that demonstrate different competencies.

## Suggested artifact

Publish at least three capstones with reproducible setup, architecture and threat diagrams, tests, evaluation data, failure demonstrations, decisions, runbooks, licenses, and short presentations tailored to technical and nontechnical reviewers.
