## Added foundations and controls

The independent curriculum adds explicit material where the source documents provide only a topic label or omit an operational prerequisite:

- model-independent exercises and deterministic baselines;
- parallel Python and Node.js automation practice;
- version control, reproducibility, schemas, and idempotency;
- prompt-injection defenses and least-privilege tools;
- representative evaluation datasets and failure analysis;
- approval, rollback, incident response, and retirement;
- capstone rubrics and a public portfolio path;
- a source policy that distinguishes official evidence from marketing.

These additions do not claim equivalence with either commercial program, academic credit, supervised teaching, or provider certification. They make the public self-study path testable and safer.
