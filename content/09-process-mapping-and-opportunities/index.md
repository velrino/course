# 09. Process Mapping and Opportunities

Observe a real process, set its boundaries, model the current state, and identify automation opportunities without hiding exceptions or human work. Feasibility, value, risk, stakeholder evidence, and measurable baselines lead to an automation brief.

## Before you begin

Complete the foundation modules through [Module 08](../08-model-context-protocol/index.md), or bring equivalent systems literacy. Select a recurring process you can observe without exposing confidential or personal data.

## Lessons

1. [Process Boundaries and the Current State](01-process-boundaries-and-current-state.md)
2. [BPMN and Service Blueprints](02-bpmn-and-service-blueprints.md)
3. [Opportunity Discovery and Prioritization](03-opportunity-discovery-and-prioritization.md)
4. [Feasibility, Value, and Metrics](04-feasibility-value-and-metrics.md)
5. [Stakeholders, Risks, and the Automation Brief](05-stakeholders-risks-and-automation-brief.md)

## Suggested artifact

Produce an evidence-backed current-state map, exception inventory, service blueprint, scored opportunity matrix, baseline metrics, stakeholder map, risk register, and one-page automation brief.

## Continue

Convert the brief into an implementable specification in [Module 10: Use-Case Design](../10-use-case-design/index.md).
