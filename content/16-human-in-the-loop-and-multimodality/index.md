# 16. Human-in-the-Loop and Multimodality

Design oversight that changes outcomes rather than merely recording approval. Durable state, review interfaces, feedback, escalation, local multimodal pipelines, accessibility, quality evaluation, and safety controls make human authority operational.

## Before you begin

Complete [Module 14](../14-agent-architecture/index.md); [Module 15](../15-rag-knowledge-sources-and-memory/index.md) provides a useful grounded system to review. Choose a reversible scenario without sensitive media.
