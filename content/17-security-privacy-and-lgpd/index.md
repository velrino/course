# 17. Security, Privacy, and LGPD

Apply threat modeling, privacy engineering, LGPD concepts, vendor and transfer analysis, security testing, and incident response to AI automation. Legal interpretation remains jurisdiction-specific; the engineering goal is explicit data flow, authority, controls, and evidence.

## Before you begin

Complete [Module 16](../16-human-in-the-loop-and-multimodality/index.md) and bring an architecture and data-flow diagram. Use synthetic data and seek qualified legal advice for real deployments.
