# 27. Project Management, Adoption, and Change

Take an AI initiative from discovery through outcomes, roadmap, backlog, technical challenges, stakeholder and work design, pilots, rollout, operations, value measurement, and continuous improvement. Adoption is measured as changed work with controlled risk.

## Before you begin

Complete [Module 26](../26-ethics-governance-impact-and-risk/index.md) and bring a governed use case, baseline process measures, named stakeholders, and known uncertainty.
