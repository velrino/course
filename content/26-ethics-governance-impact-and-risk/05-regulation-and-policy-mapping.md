# Regulation and Policy Mapping

## Learning objectives

Build a jurisdiction-aware obligations map, distinguish law from voluntary guidance, and know when specialist legal review is required.

## Start with context, not a universal checklist

Applicable obligations depend on jurisdiction, sector, role in the value chain, data, affected people, and how an output is used. Record where the provider, deployer, users, and affected people are located; whether personal, biometric, health, financial, employment, education, or children's data is involved; and whether the system influences rights or access to essential services.

Separate sources by authority:

- binding legislation and regulations;
- regulator guidance and enforcement decisions;
- contracts and internal policies;
- standards and voluntary frameworks;
- research or industry practice.

The European Union AI Act, Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, uses a risk-based structure and assigns different duties to actors such as providers and deployers. The official text and later amendments or implementing measures control; a course summary does not. Brazil's LGPD governs personal-data processing and the ANPD publishes regulatory guidance. Sector rules and consumer, employment, copyright, cybersecurity, and discrimination law may also apply even when no AI-specific rule is named.

## Obligations map

Create one row per possible obligation with source, article or section, jurisdiction, triggering fact, responsible role, required evidence, deadline, status, and legal owner. Link it to technical controls, but do not ask engineers to make legal determinations outside their competence.

Monitor changes. Record an access date and review cadence for official sources. Proposals, press releases, and consolidated law are different artifacts. Label them accurately.

## Free local practice

For a fictional recruitment assistant operating in Brazil and serving an EU office, create a preliminary issue map covering AI classification, personal data, automated decisions, transparency, security, accessibility, employment, vendor contracts, and appeals. Mark every item that needs qualified legal interpretation. Do not conclude that the project is compliant.

## Completion criteria

- Jurisdiction and value-chain roles are explicit.
- Binding and voluntary sources are not conflated.
- Each obligation maps to evidence and an owner.
- The document identifies unresolved legal questions and current-source review dates.
