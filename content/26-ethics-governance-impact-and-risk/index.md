# 26. Ethics, Governance, Impact, and Risk

Turn ethical reasoning and human-rights principles into decisions, ownership, gates, evidence, impact assessment, risk treatment, transparency, audit records, and policy mapping. Governance is treated as an operating system for responsible delivery.

## Before you begin

Complete [Modules 14-18](../14-agent-architecture/index.md) and bring a real or synthetic system architecture. Regulatory summaries are educational; obtain qualified advice for actual legal obligations.
