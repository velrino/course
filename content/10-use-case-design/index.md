# 10. Use-Case Design

Turn process evidence into a precise, testable service concept. The module connects user needs, problem framing, stories, acceptance criteria, scenarios, low-fidelity prototypes, usability evidence, and implementation handoff.

## Before you begin

Bring the automation brief from [Module 09](../09-process-mapping-and-opportunities/index.md), including its current-state evidence and risk assumptions. Do not begin from a preferred tool.

## Lessons

1. [Problem Framing and User Needs](01-problem-framing-and-user-needs.md)
2. [User Stories and Acceptance Criteria](02-user-stories-and-acceptance-criteria.md)
3. [Storyboards and Service Scenarios](03-storyboards-and-service-scenarios.md)
4. [Low-Fidelity Prototypes and Tests](04-low-fidelity-prototypes-and-tests.md)
5. [Use-Case Specification and Handoff](05-use-case-specification-and-handoff.md)

## Suggested artifact

Deliver a use-case packet containing evidence-based needs, non-goals, user stories, failure-oriented acceptance tests, an exception storyboard, a tested prototype, data boundaries, metrics, and handoff questions.
