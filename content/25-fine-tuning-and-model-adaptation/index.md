# 25. Fine-Tuning and Model Adaptation

Choose among prompting, retrieval, tools, workflow changes, and model adaptation based on diagnosed failure evidence. Then design lawful datasets, perform supervised and parameter-efficient tuning, evaluate regressions, and plan release and rollback.
