# 22. Data Analysis and Statistics

Move from a decision question and provenance record through cleaning, exploratory statistics, visualization, uncertainty, hypothesis testing, experiments, regression, and reproducible reporting. Claims must remain proportional to design and evidence.

Publish a reproducible analysis with a question contract, data dictionary, provenance and cleaning log, exploratory tables, accessible charts, uncertainty intervals, sensitivity checks, justified inferential method, limitations, and a decision memo.
