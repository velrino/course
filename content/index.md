# Free AI Automation and Agent Engineering Curriculum

Build practical foundations in AI, automation, data, and responsible agent engineering through original lessons, free-first exercises, and portfolio projects. The course is designed for independent study: no paid model, API, course subscription, or account is required for the core path.

![An AI automation system connects governed data and retrieval to a model, narrow tools, workflow control, human approval, business systems, and operational monitoring.](assets/images/ai-automation-course-system.png)

The model is only one component. The course develops the surrounding data, tool, control, evaluation, and human decision boundaries needed for dependable automation.

Use the [canonical course index](00-course-index.md) to choose a path, browse the [executable practice labs](labs.md), or begin with [Module 00: Course Orientation and Study Plan](00-course-orientation-and-study-plan/index.md). Your completion state is stored only in this browser and is never sent to a server.

## Course stages

1. **Foundations:** Modules 00-08 establish study practice, digital systems, AI, LLMs, web protocols, containers, and MCP.
2. **Automation design:** Modules 09-14 turn processes into tested workflows and bounded agent architectures.
3. **Reliable agent systems:** Modules 15-19 cover retrieval, oversight, privacy, security, evaluation, and orchestration.
4. **Code and data:** Modules 20-25 and 29 build Python, SQL, analysis, framework, adaptation, and Node.js skills.
5. **Responsible delivery:** Modules 26-28 connect governance, adoption, capstones, and portfolio evidence.

## Start deliberately

Read the [course-use guide](00-course-orientation-and-study-plan/01-how-to-use-this-course.md), prepare the [free local environment](00-course-orientation-and-study-plan/04-free-local-environment.md), and keep evidence for every completion criterion. The course rewards tested artifacts and explanations, not passive reading.
