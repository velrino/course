# 24. CrewAI

Distinguish Crews from Flows and decide when role specialization adds measurable value. The module covers agents, tasks, processes, state, control, tools, memory, knowledge, guardrails, and an evidence-grounded research project.

## Before you begin

Complete [Module 23](../23-langchain-and-langgraph/index.md) or bring equivalent agent-framework experience. Retain a single-process baseline and a small corpus with clear rights.

## Suggested artifact

Create a bounded research Flow and Crew with typed task outputs, narrow tools, citation checks, explicit state, stop conditions, adversarial prompts, cost and latency measures, and a quality comparison against one simpler implementation.
